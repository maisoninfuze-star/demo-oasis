# Fabric Suppliers — Galerie Oasis
*Identified from owner's showroom sample books (13 photos, 2026-08-03) + web research.*

## 1. Ennis Fabrics (J. Ennis Fabrics Ltd.) — PRIMARY, BEST DIGITAL ASSETS
- **Who:** Canada's major wholesale fabric distributor. Founded 1972, HQ Edmonton; DCs in Toronto, Vancouver, Indianapolis; 9,000+ products. Exclusive Canadian distributor of Ultrafabrics.
- **Web:** ennisfabrics.com (public Shopify catalog with per-colorway swatch images; pricing behind dealer login)
- **Books in the store:**
  - **Harper** — solid upholstery, textured weave, contract rated. 100% polyester, 56" width, 145,000 double rubs (Wyzenbeek), cleaning code W. 13 colorways (Mineral 99, Oyster 608, Pewter 92, Cinder 97, Indigo 308, Poppy 14 current; Cypress 209, Amber 47, Sable 809, Slate 903, Meadow 205, Charcoal 908, Ginger 406 discontinued). + **Endurepel Harper** (stain-defense treated) 6 colorways.
  - **Franklin 2nd Edition** — velvet look, contract rated. ~28 colorways (Petrol 308, Fawn 67, Snow 61, Mink 609, Camel 47, Deep Purple 1008, Blush 105, Orchid 108, Theatre Red 14, Sage 202 + more). + **Endurepel Franklin** ~11 colorways (Imperial Blue 306, Cinder 97, Midnight 3009…). *Owner hand-wrote "ORDER 2" on this book — active line for them.*
  - **Heavenly 3rd Edition** — plush residential upholstery. **47 colorways** (Burgundy 1006, Plum 1008, Aubergine 1009, Coral 11, Mulberry 17 + 42 more). + **Endurepel Heavenly** 17 colorways.
- **Integration path:** owner's dealer account → digital swatch assets + pricing; public product pages already expose swatch imagery per colorway. Ennis is the ideal first supplier to wire into the website fabric selector.

## 2. A Class Upholstery Ltd (ACU) — FURNITURE MAKER + FABRIC PROGRAM
- **Who:** Canadian upholstered-furniture manufacturer since 1995. 7635 Kimbel St, Mississauga ON L5S 1M6 · (905) 595-2330 · info@aclassfurniture.ca · aclassfurniture.ca
- **Catalog:** 81 sofas, 27 sectionals, 29 chairs, 11 ottomans, 6 chaises, 3 sofa beds (site lists items; no public fabric catalog).
- **Books in the store:**
  - **Iconic Collection Vol. I — Performance, Woven Texture**
  - **Iconic Collection Vol. II — Performance, Velvet Texture**
  - Several white-label ACU swatch books (boucle, velvet, geometric jacquard, etc.)
  - "Performance Fabrics by ACU": stain & soil release, 99.9% bacterial reduction, easy-clean, moisture barrier, no formaldehyde/phthalates, 95% water-based tech.
- **Meaning:** Galerie Oasis orders ACU furniture made-to-order in these fabrics. Fabric options belong ON the product pages of ACU-made pieces.
- **Integration path:** no public API/images — request digital swatch scans + fabric list from ACU rep (or we scan the physical books at the store; ~30 min with a phone rig).
